What keeps Indeed inside the rules

Four requests maximum across both countries and every role, one lease at a time, a fixed delay between requests and a cooldown after a normal finish. No timing randomisation, no user-agent spoofing, no proxies. The caps are the entire mechanism, and they are published on the sources page rather than tuned to avoid notice.
