Where the jobs come from.

Public advertisements only. No logins, no detection evasion. Reading a source and republishing its advertisement text are separate questions, and this page answers both.

How collection is limited

4
requests per source, per run
400ms
fixed pause between requests
1
run at a time, per source
0
accounts used to read a page

Pacing is fixed, never randomised. Nothing here disguises what this application is or how often it asks. If a source does not want to be read, the answer is to stop reading it.

Authorized APIs

Company career boards

Greenhouse, Lever, Recruitee, Ashby, Personio — each read through its own published API.

EURES (Switzerland and Netherlands)

Reused with the permission of the European Labour Authority, acknowledged as the source of that material.

Job-Room / arbeit.swiss

The Swiss public employment service, read through its published interface.

Not used, and why

Nationale Vacaturebank

No interface that permits it. Reading the pages anyway would be the thing this product does not do.

werk.nl / UWV

The same. The absence is deliberate, not an oversight.

LinkedIn

Its terms forbid it outright, and no amount of care would change that.

werkzoeken.nl, magnet.me, youngcapital.nl, jobbird.com

Aggregators of the above. Reading them would be reading those sources through a side door.
