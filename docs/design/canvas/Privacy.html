Your job search stays yours.

What is never collected, what is stored and why, and how to get rid of all of it.

What is never collected

No analytics account, no advertising identifier, no behavioural profile, nothing sold or shared. The only thing counted about visitors is how many came on a given day.

Visits are counted as a number per day and a number of distinct visitors per day. There is no row per person, so the count cannot be turned back into who visited.

What is stored, and why

Your email address and a hash of your password

To let you back in. The password itself is never stored.

The advertisements you have collected, and your notes

This is the product. It is scoped to your account by every query that touches it.

Your search settings: roles, required and excluded keywords

So a search does the same thing twice.

The listings your searches judged and set aside

So the same advertisement is not offered to you again every week.

Sign-in records: the email tried, the IP, and whether it succeeded

To make a password guessed at scale expensive. Deleted with the account.

A daily count of visits and distinct visitors

Two numbers a day. Nothing per person.

Cookies

ike_session

One cookie, holding your session. There is no second cookie, no consent banner, and nothing to decline — because nothing optional is set.

Your rights

Access and portability

Ask and you get what is held, in a form you can take elsewhere.

Rectification

Anything wrong gets corrected.

Erasure

Settings has a delete that removes the account and everything attached to it.

Restriction and objection

Processing can be limited or objected to.

Complaints

To a supervisory authority, without going through us first.

Where the data lives

One database per environment, and the environments do not share. Development and test hold synthetic data only; nothing real has ever been in them.
